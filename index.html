SKIN LIBRARY • ACNE

Understanding Acne

Acne is one of the most common skin concerns, especially during the teen years. It can happen when hair follicles become clogged with oil and dead skin cells.

♡

What Causes Acne?

Hormonal changes can increase oil production. Extra oil, dead skin cells, and bacteria can contribute to clogged pores and breakouts.

✦

Common Types

Acne can include blackheads, whiteheads, pimples, and deeper inflamed breakouts.

♡

Caring for Acne-Prone Skin

Gentle cleansing and avoiding aggressive scrubbing can help prevent unnecessary irritation.

SkinSmart Tip

Try not to pick or squeeze pimples. Doing so can irritate the skin and may increase the chance of marks or scarring.

When should you see a dermatologist?

Consider talking with a dermatologist if your acne is painful, severe, leaving scars or dark marks, or isn't improving.

SKIN LIBRARY • SUN PROTECTION

Why Sun Protection Matters

Protecting your skin from ultraviolet radiation is an important part of long-term skin health.

☀

What Is SPF?

SPF stands for Sun Protection Factor. It describes the level of protection sunscreen provides against UVB radiation.

✦

Broad-Spectrum

Broad-spectrum sunscreen protects against both UVA and UVB radiation.

☀

More Than Sunscreen

Shade, protective clothing, hats, and avoiding tanning beds can also help reduce UV exposure.

SkinSmart Tip

Look for a broad-spectrum, water-resistant sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher.

SKIN LIBRARY • INGREDIENTS

Skincare Ingredients 101

Skincare labels can be confusing. Here's a simple introduction to several commonly discussed ingredients.

Salicylic Acid

Acne

An ingredient commonly used to help unclog pores and treat certain types of acne.

Benzoyl Peroxide

Acne

An acne treatment that can help reduce acne-causing bacteria and inflammation.

Ceramides

Skin Barrier

Lipids that help support the skin barrier and help the skin retain moisture.

Hyaluronic Acid

Hydration

A hydrating ingredient that helps attract and hold water in the skin.

Adapalene

Acne

A topical retinoid commonly used to help treat acne and unclog pores.

Glycerin

Hydration

A humectant that helps attract water to the outer layer of skin.

Important

Some active ingredients can cause dryness or irritation. Introduce new products carefully and learn what is appropriate for your skin.

DERMATOLOGY

When should you see a dermatologist?

Dermatologists are doctors who specialize in conditions involving the skin, hair, and nails.

✓ Acne that is painful or severe
✓ Acne that is leaving scars or dark marks
✓ Skin irritation that does not improve
✓ A changing or concerning skin spot
✓ Questions about a persistent skin problem

A dermatologist can examine your skin, determine what may be causing a concern, and recommend appropriate treatment.
